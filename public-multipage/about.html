About the House

加班

A House of Quiet Prestige

加班 is envisioned as a dining house of restraint, atmosphere, and cultivated taste. Its identity is rooted in low-lit elegance, composed presentation, and an experience that unfolds with confidence rather than spectacle. The house favors depth over noise, precision over ornament, and permanence over trend.

Our Philosophy

We believe refinement is best expressed through control—measured flavors, intentional design, and service that is felt more than announced. Every element within 加班 is guided by clarity, mood, and a sense of ceremonial calm.

The Dining Experience

An evening at 加班 is designed to move with quiet rhythm. Courses arrive with purpose. Textures and aromas unfold in layers. The room remains intimate, the energy composed, and the overall impression one of polished discretion.

Atmosphere
Low-lit, intimate, and elegantly restrained
Cuisine Direction
Composed, expressive, and detail-driven
Service Style
Discreet, poised, and highly attentive
Signature Mood
Warm shadow, subtle drama, and lasting refinement
House Note

加班 is created for guests who appreciate the language of detail—those who notice finish, pacing, light, texture, and the quiet confidence of a well-composed room. It is a place where dining becomes atmosphere, and atmosphere becomes memory.

Curated evenings. Composed plates. Private prestige.
